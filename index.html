Ibn Ul Haitham Computer Architecture Research Lab

Building the next generation of intelligent computing systems.

HICOAR Lab explores computer architecture, efficient AI hardware, digital systems, VLSI design, and hardware–software co-design for high-performance and energy-efficient computing.

Explore our research Work with us
Our mission

Re-imagining computing from microarchitecture to physical implementation.

We investigate how processors and specialized accelerators can become faster, more energy efficient, more secure, and easier to design—while giving students the tools to turn research ideas into working systems.

Computer Architecture

AI & Domain-Specific Accelerators
