HICOAR LAB · UET LAHORE

Ibn Ul HaithamComputer ArchitectureResearch Lab

HICOAR explores computer architecture, efficient AI hardware, digital systems, VLSI design, and hardware–software co-design for high-performance and energy-efficient computing.

From microarchitecture to physical implementation, we turn ideas into measurable systems and train students to build, analyze, and improve future computing platforms.

Explore Research Project Hub
Our mission

Re-imagining computing from microarchitecture to physical implementation.

We investigate how processors and specialized accelerators can become faster, more energy efficient, more secure, and easier to design—while giving students the tools to turn research ideas into working systems.
