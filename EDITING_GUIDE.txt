HICOAR LAB — QUICK EDITING GUIDE

FILES
-----
index.html   = page content. Each major section has a clear comment.
styles.css  = all colors, fonts, sizes and layout.
script.js   = mobile-menu and footer-year behavior.
logo.png    = place your existing HICOAR logo in this same folder.

FASTEST CHANGES
---------------
1. Open styles.css.
2. At the very top, edit the values under QUICK EDIT SETTINGS.

Important variables:
--hero-maroon: #6E1F28;    First-section solid background
--maroon: #6F1D2A;         Main maroon
--yellow: #F6D65B;         Main yellow
--nav-logo-width: 190px;   Navigation logo width
--nav-logo-scale: 8;       Navigation logo scale
--section-padding: 96px;   Vertical spacing between sections

#home          First / hero section
Mission        Our mission section
#research      Research areas
